MODULE 5
Split Bill Engine

Implement the payment workflow exactly as designed.

Rules:

One customer owns the session.

At payment:

Display:

"How many additional contributors will help pay?"

The owner enters the number.

Generate secure payment tokens.

The table QR temporarily allows ONLY that number of contributors.

Each contributor scans the QR.

They DO NOT enter the LoungeOS dashboard.

Instead they see ONLY:

• Contribution amount
• Phone number
• Confirm table number
• Confirm Till Number
• Pay

Each payment updates the owner's dashboard instantly.

The owner sees:

Paid
Pending
Outstanding Balance

Waiter confirms final settlement.

Session closes only after the remaining balance reaches zero.

Tokens expire after payment or timeout.

Acceptance:
No contributor can access the lounge session or order food.
