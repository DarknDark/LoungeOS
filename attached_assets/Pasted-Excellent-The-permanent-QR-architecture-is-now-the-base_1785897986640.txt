Real-time Updates

Where practical, use Firestore listeners or an equivalent real-time mechanism so waiter dashboards and customer dashboards stay synchronized.

Examples:

Waiter adds a drink → customer bill updates immediately.
Customer places an order → waiter sees it immediately.
Waiter confirms payment → customer sees payment confirmed.
Waiter closes table → customer session closes automatically and the permanent QR becomes available again.

Avoid polling unless there is no suitable real-time alternative.
