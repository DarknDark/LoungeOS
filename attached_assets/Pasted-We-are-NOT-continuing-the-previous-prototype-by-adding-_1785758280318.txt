IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY

Do not attempt to build every feature in one pass.

First, review the current codebase and present a refactoring plan that aligns it with this architecture.

Then implement features in small, testable phases. After each phase:

Verify type safety.
Verify mobile preview.
Verify role permissions.
Verify database interactions.
Summarize what changed before moving to the next phase.

The goal is not just to have a working prototype—it is to build a maintainable, production-ready nightclub operating platform that can grow with the business and support additional clubs in the future.
